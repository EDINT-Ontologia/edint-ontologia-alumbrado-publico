Energy Consumptionc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: http://vocab.linkeddata.es/datosabiertos/def/urbanismo-infraestructuras/alumbrado-publico#ConsumoEnergetico

Observation of electricity consumption in a specific tariff period. By inheriting from edintsum:UtilityConsumption ⊂ sosa:Observation, uses the SOSA pattern: – sosa:hasFeatureOfInterest → the CentroDeMando (supply point) – sosa:observedProperty → edintsum:electricityConsumption – sosa:hasResult → qudt:QuantityValue (qudt:numericValue + qudt:hasUnit) – edintalu:periodoTarifario → SKOS concept (active, standard, off-peak, peak) Each instance represents consumption in ONE tariff period. The total is obtained by summing instances of the same fiscal year.
has super-classes

is in range of

Streetlightc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: http://vocab.linkeddata.es/datosabiertos/def/urbanismo-infraestructuras/alumbrado-publico#Farola

A complete point of light, composed of a post, one or more luminaires (which in turn contain lamps) and optionally a junction box. It is an urban element (edintinf:UrbanElement) and, by inheritance, an InfrastructureElement that can have a utility supply point (edintsum:hasUtilitySupplyPoint). Every instance of Farola has infrastructure type 'Street lamp' (edintkosinftype:StreeLamp) from the EDINT SKOS.
